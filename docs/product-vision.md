# IFEXA GO — Product Vision

## 1. Overview

IFEXA GO is a local commerce and delivery platform being developed to connect customers, vendors, sourcing agents, and delivery riders.

## 2. Problem We Are Solving

Customers may struggle to discover products from local vendors, source items that are not readily available online, and arrange convenient delivery.

Vendors may also need better digital tools to reach customers and manage orders.

## 3. Our Vision

To make discovering, sourcing, ordering, and delivering products from local businesses simpler through one digital platform.

## 4. Target Market

Initial market: Onitsha, Anambra State, Nigeria.

Target users:
- Customers looking for products.
- Vendors selling products.
- Agents helping customers source products.
- Riders responsible for deliveries.
- Administrators managing platform operations.

## 5. Proposed Core Features

- Customer registration and accounts.
- Vendor registration and product listings.
- Product discovery and search.
- Customer product requests and agent-assisted sourcing.
- Order management.
- Rider assignment and delivery tracking.
- Administrative tools for managing operations.

These are proposed features, not claims that they have already been implemented.

## 6. Product Leadership

IFEXA product leadership is responsible for defining the product vision, gathering user feedback, prioritising requirements, coordinating the engineering team, reviewing progress, and documenting product decisions.

Engineers are responsible for implementing and documenting their technical work.

## 7. Current Stage

Product planning and development.

The MVP scope, technical implementation, and launch readiness will be documented as decisions are made and work is verified.
