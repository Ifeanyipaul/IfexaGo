# IFEXA GO

## About the Product

IFEXA GO is a local commerce and delivery platform being developed to connect customers, vendors, agents, and delivery riders.

## The Problem

Customers may struggle to find products, compare available options, and arrange reliable delivery from local vendors.

## Our Approach

IFEXA GO aims to simplify product discovery, product sourcing, ordering, and delivery through a single digital platform.

## Core Features

- Customer registration and authentication
- Vendor registration and product listings
- Product search and ordering
- Agent-assisted product sourcing
- Rider assignment and delivery tracking
- Order management and customer support

## Product Development

The product is being developed by an engineering team under the direction of IFEXA's product leadership.

This repository documents product requirements, development decisions, engineering progress, testing, and improvements.

## Current Status

Planning and product development.

Features will be marked as complete only after implementation and verification.
